## How to support

You can support Reactive Resume by one of many ways:
- by suggesting features or report bugs on GitHub
- by helping to translate the application (and improving existing translations) on Crowdin
- by donating/sponsoring the project on GitHub or Open Collective

## A small brief about your project

It's fairly simple. A free, open-source, privacy-first resume builder developed because there are too many alternatives that are behind a sneaky paywall. Also that most open-source software, at least at the time, was associated with subpar design, and Reactive Resume aimed to change that perception.
